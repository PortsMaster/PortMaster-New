Put one of these here:

GOG:
  Copy the Linux offline installer (deponia_4_0_1386_90850.sh or similar) here as it is.
  It is unpacked on the first launch (takes a few minutes, needs about 2 GB of free space), then deleted.

Steam (app 214340):
  The Linux version is two depots, you need both:
    214358  Linux deponia base      (game data, 1.5 GB)
    214359  Linux deponia binaries  (Deponia, libs64)

  With the Steam client: open the console (steam://open/console in a browser or the Run dialog), then enter
    download_depot 214340 214358
    download_depot 214340 214359
  Steam saves them in steamapps/content/app_214340/depot_214358 and depot_214359.

  Or with DepotDownloader:
    DepotDownloader -app 214340 -depot 214358 214359 -os linux -username <your Steam name> -dir Deponia

  Copy the contents of both depots together into this folder, so that Deponia, data.vis, libs64,
  scenes and videos sit directly in gamedata.
