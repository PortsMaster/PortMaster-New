# party-bars

While you walk, the bars of the menu on the map: one member of the party with
Hunger (ochre), Body (blood red) and Mind (blue grey), in the order of the
menu. Hunger works as in the menu: empty when not hungry, it fills the
hungrier you are. At the top left, with no background and see-through so you
can still see the enemies behind it. L1 and R1 go to the previous or the next member.
It only shows while you can walk: not in events, scenes, messages or loading.

It comes on. To turn it off: `party-bars: Off` in the Mods option of the
menu of the port.
