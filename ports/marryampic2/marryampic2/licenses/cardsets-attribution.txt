Marryampic2 cardset attribution
================================

Attribution below is the header-comment text from each cardset's own
cardinfo.txt, as shipped by its original author, unedited beyond whitespace
cleanup. No license is invented for a cardset that doesn't state one.

  - LaBalladeDesDalton, CartoonsThe80s, Musical_Instruments
Legacy Marryampic I cardsets (from Aminet, aminet.net/game/data/, and the
legacy cardsets page's own mirrored Drive folder for 6 of them):
  - all others below
