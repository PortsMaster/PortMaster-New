MMPX, the pixel-art magnification filter (src/sorp.c), follows the
reference code of Morgan McGuire and Mara Gagiu, "MMPX Style-Preserving
Pixel Art Magnification", Journal of Computer Graphics Techniques 10(2),
2021: https://casual-effects.com/research/McGuire2021PixelArt/
