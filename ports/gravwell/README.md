## Controls

| Button | Action |
|--|--|
|D-Pad|Move|
|Face buttons|Fire in that direction; two neighbouring buttons fire the diagonal|
|Right stick|Aim and fire, 8 ways|
|L1/R1/L2/R2|Dash|
|Start or Select|Pause, and see your haul|
|A|Confirm|
|B|Back / reroll|

OPTIONS on the title menu holds DISPLAY (Pixel perfect / Fit / Fill), the music and sound-effect mutes, the controls list and CLEAR SAVE. All of it is remembered between sessions.

On Knulli, enable the A/B X/Y swap option.
