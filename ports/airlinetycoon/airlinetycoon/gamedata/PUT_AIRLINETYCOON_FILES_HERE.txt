Put one of these here:

GOG:
  Copy the Linux offline installer (gog_airline_tycoon_deluxe_2.0.0.9.sh or similar) here as it is.
  It is unpacked on the first launch (takes a few minutes, needs about 1 GB of free space), then deleted.

Steam (app 331920):
  The Linux version is a single depot:
    331922  Airline Tycoon Deluxe Linux

  With the Steam client: open the console (steam://open/console in a browser or the Run dialog), then enter
    download_depot 331920 331922
  Steam saves it in steamapps/content/app_331920/depot_331922.

  Or with DepotDownloader:
    DepotDownloader -app 331920 -depot 331922 -os linux -username <your Steam name> -dir AirlineTycoonDeluxe

  Copy the contents of the depot into this folder, so that ATDeluxe, lib32, room, sound, video, en, de
  and fr sit directly in gamedata. The .DepotDownloader folder and the PDF manuals are not needed, they
  are deleted on the first launch.
