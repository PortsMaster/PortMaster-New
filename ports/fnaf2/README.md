## Controls

| Button | Action |
|--|--| 
|start|enter/confirm|
|L1|left light|
|L2|mask|
|R1|right light|
|R2|cameras|
|left analog left| pan office left|
|left analog right| pan office right|
|Y| mute call|
|A|flashlight|
|X|wind music box
|D-pad|navigate cameras|
