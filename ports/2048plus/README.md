This project is inspired by and references the popular open-source [2048](https://github.com/tpcstld/2048) by tpcstld, which itself is based on the original web game by Gabriele Cirulli. While drawing visual and design inspiration from tpcstld's implementation, this codebase was written from the ground up in Lua for the LÖVE engine. In addition to the classic gameplay, it introduces numerous new features, including multiple game modes, a store, an achievement system, and a wide variety of themes to enhance the overall experience.

Source Code: [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/saitamasahil/2048plus)

## Features

- **Multiple Game Modes**: Classic 2048, Plus mode with powerups like Undo, Swap, and Bomb, and Arcade modes including Time Attack, 5x5 Huge, No Mercy, and Goose.
- **Theme Studio**: Design and save your own custom themes by picking base tones, styles, hues, and vibrancy, with a live board preview and quick randomizer.
- **Customization & Companions**: Unlock lots of themes, board materials like wood and marble, and animated cat and dog companions that hang out while you play.
- **In-Game Store**: Earn coins just by playing and use them to unlock themes, board skins, and upgrades.
- **Achievements & Stats**: Plenty of achievements to unlock and stats tracking your high scores and records.
- **Jukebox & Wi-Fi Music Manager**: Listen to background music with an audio visualizer, or easily upload your own songs from your phone or PC over local Wi-Fi.
- **Built for Handhelds**: Auto-saves after every move, smooth controller navigation, haptics, and fast theme switching.

*Note: There might also be a secret hidden behind a classic button sequence...*

## Controls

| Action | Button |
|:---|:---|
| **Move Tiles** | D-Pad / Left Stick |
| **Confirm / Select Item** | `A` |
| **Undo Move / Back / Cancel** | `B` |
| **Quit to Menu** | `X` |
| **Cycle Unlocked Themes** | `Y` |
| **Activate Swap Powerup** | `L1` |
| **Activate Bomb Powerup** | `R1` |
| **Seek Music** | D-Pad Left / Right |
| **Show Coin Balance** | `Select` |
| **Pause Menu / Resume** | `Start` |
| **Force Quit App** | `Menu + Start` |

*Note: Your progress is automatically saved after every move. You can safely close the game and pick up exactly where you left off.*
