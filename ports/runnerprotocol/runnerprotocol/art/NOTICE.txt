RUNNER PROTOCOL baked assets.

maratype.fnt is baked from Maratype (tools/Maratype.otf), a display typeface
by imago? / metamorphosiis, released freely: no commercial restrictions,
redistribution allowed, attribution encouraged. The baker (tools/bake_font.py)
synthesizes the glyphs the face doesn't carry (%, <, >) and redraws X/V/K for
small sizes.

The sfx_*.wav files are synthesized from arithmetic by tools/bake_sfx.py.

The *.raw art is this project's own baked artwork.
