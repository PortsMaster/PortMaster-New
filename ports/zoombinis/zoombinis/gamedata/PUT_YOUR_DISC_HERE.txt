Put your Logical Journey of the Zoombinis Windows CD here: an .iso, a .bin/.cue, or all the files on the CD.
The game installs itself from it on first launch; afterwards these files can be deleted.
A CD with several languages installs English; to choose another, put a language.txt here naming it, e.g. german or french.
To reinstall from another CD later, delete ports/zoombinis/game and launch again (saved games are kept).
