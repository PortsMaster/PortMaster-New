## Notes

Many years after the events of Crop and Claw have passed. A hermit dragon by name of Wend and his friends set sail to a far away continent in search of a new home. But an ominous wind carries news that Wend's past has come to haunt him, even across the ocean itself.

# Features

* Build a custom party of dragons and join story characters throughout an adventure.
* Dragons, everywhere.
* A non-linear adventure allowing for exploration, routing, and replay-ability.
* You are a dragon.
* Turn-based combat against flora and dragons alike.
* Multiple interactions and solutions to puzzles with characters and environments, encouraging exploration and experimentation.
* Dragons. It really is a dragon adventure.

## Demo
* Contains the first act of the full game, for roughly one to two hours of play.
* Allows for both story party and custom mercenaries.
* Save compatibility for the full game of Crop and Claw 2 is not guaranteed!
