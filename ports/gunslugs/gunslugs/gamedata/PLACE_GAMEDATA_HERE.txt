Place either:
- gunslugs.dat from the supported GOG Windows Gunslugs 3.3.0 build, or
- your backed-up Gunslugs 3.2.4 APK (versionCode 52).

Launch Gunslugs from Ports. If both inputs exist, PC gunslugs.dat takes priority.
Preparation runs on the handheld with a PortMaster progress bar.
PC output: pc/GAME.JAR. APK output: GAME.JAR and assets/.
Original inputs are retained; later launches reuse prepared data.
See README.md for supported hashes, installation and controls.
