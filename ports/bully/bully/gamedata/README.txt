Bully: Anniversary Edition Final - BYO game data
=================================================

Put your complete, legal v1.4.311 arm64 source in this directory.

Documented sources:
  - one complete merged APK; or
  - every APK from a fully purchased Play Store installation, including the
    base, arm64 and all split_data_* APKs; or
  - one complete .apks, .apkm or .xapk export.

Do not copy only split_data_1.apk. Do not use an export from the limited trial.
Keep at least 6 GB free for source plus transactional staging.
After a fully validated setup, only copied APK/split/bundle sources that
actually supplied selected libraries or data are deleted to reclaim space.
The release package intentionally contains no libGame.so, libc++_shared.so,
Rockstar runtime assets or data archives.
