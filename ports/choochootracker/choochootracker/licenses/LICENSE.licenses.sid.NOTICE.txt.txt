SID native engine: floooh/chips m6581, zlib license. See chips-LICENSE and
chips-NOTICE.txt for origin and implementation modifications.

ChooChoo SID Originals: 32 project-authored parameter programs, repository MIT
license. Eight families with four variants each; no game extractions.

SIDkit Effects: 24 adapted programs from c64SIDkit, MIT license, copyright
2026 c64SIDkit contributors. Source tree fc541093c5cc3ae3664a36a274c3c3f938de2d07.
https://github.com/devinvenable/c64SIDkit
The adapter preserves native oscillator/envelope parameters and implements
pitch/filter sweeps and vibrato. Fixed-pitch effects and sustained effects are
marked by their program parameters. These are not verified game recreations.

The CC-BY instrument candidates in tools/sid_prep are not shipped in these banks.
