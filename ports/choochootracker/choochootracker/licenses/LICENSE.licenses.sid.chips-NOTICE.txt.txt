ChooChooTracker modifications: corrected the triangle+saw+pulse combination's
logical AND to bitwise AND. The SIDVoice adapter uses the digital oscillator,
hardware envelope and per-cycle filter helpers for a single audible oscillator,
clocking a silent partner only for ring/sync. It omits permanently silent voices
and bus-read bookkeeping. This is a generic 6581 approximation; no alternate
hardware revision or character profiles are provided.

The adapter initializes the shared cutoff table once off the audio callback,
then clones immutable initial state for independent song/export contexts.
