OpenChaos PortMaster SDL3 shim provenance

The PortMaster build checks out this exact commit in detached-HEAD mode and
verifies the resulting HEAD before configuring OpenChaos.

Required CMake options:

- OPENCHAOS_SDL3_SOURCE_DIR=<verified checkout>
- SDL_SDL2_BACKEND=ON
- SDL_UNIX_CONSOLE_BUILD=ON
- SDL_ALSA=OFF
- SDL_PIPEWIRE=OFF
- SDL_PULSEAUDIO=OFF
- SDL_X11=OFF
- SDL_WAYLAND=OFF
- SDL_KMSDRM=OFF

The packaged library has SONAME libSDL3.so.0 and delegates its platform backend
to the firmware-provided SDL2 library.
