version 1.0.3

I added some new scenarios in lesser scale to scenario pack I made recently, now it is the two scenario packs:
1) sf-campaign - bigger scale campaign made using original panzer general maps; due to its scale it is possible to make alternate, science-fiction WWII history with that (however it is more probable to lose it what is more historricaly correct). I am making a little upgrades to it (if any),
2) lesser-scale - lesser scale experimental scenario packs, where some scenarios are "bind" as two campaigns:
  a) campaign about Gerd von Rundstedt is unfinished and I have no idea (as yet) how to finish it,
  b) campaign about Stanislaw Maczek needs some corrections... (he was main commander only in Jordanow battle) ),
there are also some stand-alone scenarios. By making that pack I wanted to find out, if it wouldn't be better to implement battles in lesser scale, than in scale used most pg scenarios (and now I think it is better).

most of resources (icons, some maps) are from original PG, or Gerold Treitler's
campaign.

to install this add-on, copy it to lgeneral data dir (I guess it will be
something like one of listed below:
 /usr/share/lgeneral
 /usr/share/games/lgeneral
 /usr/local/share/lgeneral
 /usr/local/share/games/lgeneral
)
