## Notes

The Hermetic Tower is a LÖVE game by sebacamaru, packaged for PortMaster by the developer.

Fonts by VEXED (CC BY 4.0). Music and most sound effects by pegonthetrack & ELVGames, used under commercial license.

Saves and settings are stored in `thehermetictower/conf/`.

## Controls

| Button | Action |
|--|--|
|D-pad|Navigate menus|
|A|Confirm / advance text / Attack (battle)|
|B|Back / Guard (hold, battle)|
|D-pad Left / Up / Down|Suprasoul / Items / Escape (battle)|
|Start|Pause / settings|
|Select|Toggle FPS counter|
|Select + Start|Quit|
