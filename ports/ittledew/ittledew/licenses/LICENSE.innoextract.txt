innoextract (tools/innoextract; build recipe in tools/src/innoextract/build), version 1.9,
https://constexpr.org/innoextract/ - unpacks the GOG Windows installer during setup. It is linked statically against
Boost 1.74 (iostreams, filesystem, date_time, system, program_options), liblzma, zlib and bzip2; their licenses
follow innoextract's.
