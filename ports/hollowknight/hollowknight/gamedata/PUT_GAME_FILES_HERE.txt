Put your copy of Hollow Knight here, then launch Hollow Knight from the Ports menu:

  Hollow Knight for Linux (Unity 6000.0.61f1): GOG's installer as downloaded (hollow_knight_*.sh, no renaming), or the game folder from GOG or Steam.

The first launch builds the game on the handheld (one to three hours, once); it uses up the game's
folder here. Unity's Android player comes with the port (`donor/`: small games built with Unity for it, whose APKs carry the player); nothing is downloaded, no Wi-Fi. Options and help: https://github.com/wild1walker/portmaster-hollowknight
