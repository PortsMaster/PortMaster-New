Battle your way through a seedy tavern filled with gambling addicted townspeople in this Blackjack Roguelike Adventure. From aces and face cards to tarot cards, business cards and more, create your unique deck to beat the house.

Requires you to own the game and provide your own game files

To obtain the game files for this port, open Steam Console and type or paste the command 
download_depot 2400510 2400512 8521945860051414369
into the command line. The files location will be shown after complete. Copy the PCK file from here to the dndg/gamedata folder.

## Controls

Left Stick / D-pad | navigate menu 
A | select
X | Hit
Y | Stand
L2| view deck
