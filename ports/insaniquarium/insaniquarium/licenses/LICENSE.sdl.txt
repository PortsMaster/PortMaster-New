The bundled libSDL3.so.0 is built from bmdhacks/SDL's `sdl2-backend` fork
(https://github.com/bmdhacks/SDL/tree/sdl2-backend), not upstream libsdl-org/SDL
directly -- it delegates video/audio/joystick to the target device's own
already-installed SDL2 via dlopen, which is why this port needs no bundled
SDL2. On top of that fork it carries one local patch, described in the
README, without which relative pointer motion loses its sign. Same zlib
license as upstream SDL, reproduced below.
