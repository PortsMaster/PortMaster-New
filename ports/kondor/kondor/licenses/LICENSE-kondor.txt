II. How to play:

Default Keys:

Right Shift    - Fire
Space          - Break
Left Arrow     - Turn left
Right Arrow    - Turn right
Up Arrow       - Drive
Down Arrow     - Reverse 
Enter          - Use inventory
[              - Inventory left
]              - Inventory right
Z              - Turn cannon left
X              - Turn cannon right


You can change the keys through the OPTIONS menu.

Also in the OPTIONS menu (switched with spacebar):

Power bar [On/Off] - Do you want the enemies' power to show up next to them (default=yes)
Sound [On/Off] - Turns sound in the game on and off, if the game will not detect your sound blaster
this options will be always [off] and unchangeable.


Your goal in the game is to pass all the levels.
In order to pass a level you have to kill all the enemies in it: Turrets and Ground vehicles.
Jet fighters and Hawks will also attack you from the air, but you don't have to kill them.
In some levels enemy generators will make your life harder. You don't have to destroy them
too, but you should.
Bonuses are also spread on the map: Rockets, Repair Kits, Shields, Nitros (speed), Lives. They
are essential for you.

For more information about the game play reffer to our site.
