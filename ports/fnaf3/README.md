## Controls

| Button | Action |
|--|--| 
|A|enter/confirm|
|L1|System Reboot Menu|
|L2|Play Audio|
|R1|Camera Panel|
|R2|Switch cameras to vents|
|left analog left|pan office left|
|left analog right|pan office right|
|Y|mute call|
|X|Seal Vents|
|D-pad|navigate cameras|
